Good to know

Plan ahead for your Mondstadt journey.

  • Best season

    Spring and early autumn bring mild, breezy days.

  • Getting around

    Easy walking routes between the lake, plains, and city.

  • Bring

    Valid ID and your confirmation details.

  • What to wear

    Light layers and comfortable walking shoes.
